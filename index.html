mumeao: Cozy 3D Cat

Cozy cat who naps as you focus

A lifelike 3D cat lives in a small, warm room on your phone. Pet her, play with her and make her room yours. When you need to focus, she naps on your phone.

A cat with a life of her own

  • Your cat has her own temperament: bold or shy, cuddly or independent, greedy or not.
  • She naps, grooms, suns herself, kneads, gets the zoomies and chatters at birds at the window.
  • Put out a box and she will sit in it; a shy cat hides under the bed.
  • Trust grows slowly. The more she trusts you, the more she slow-blinks at you.
  • Her pupils and whiskers show how she feels.
  • She remembers her favorite toys and where she likes to be stroked.

Play with her

  • Throw toys for her to chase and pounce on, or flutter a feather wand from the shop for her to stalk.
  • She bats a crinkle ball around, then carries it off.
  • Draw a red dot with the laser pointer and she hunts it.
  • Most cats go silly for a catnip pouch. Some just sniff it.
  • Double-tap her for a close-up, or pinch to zoom in on the room.
  • Pet her and stroke her fur with your finger.
  • Call her with a "pspsps". She may come, or come later.
  • Buy her a treat jar and offer treats by hand, up to three a day.
  • Knock on the floor, or hold out your hand and wait.

Make her room yours

  • 6 wall colors, 4 wallpapers and 9 wood floors, all free.
  • 13 things to buy with coins, from a sisal scratcher to a catnip pouch.
  • Move and rotate the furniture, or put things away.
  • Draw the curtains, open the window, follow the day into night.

When you need to focus

Start a Focus session and she walks over, curls up on your phone and falls asleep. Keep the app open, put the phone face down and let her be. Pick it up and one ear flicks: she is listening. Leave the app or stop early and she wakes up and gives you a look. Don't wake the cat.

  • 25-minute focus sessions, with a 5-minute break and a longer break after every fourth.
  • A purr you can feel: the phone purrs while she sleeps.
  • Every finished session earns 50 coins to spend on her room.
  • During the break she chases a string toy you drag for her.
  • After a few finished sessions, the spot by your phone becomes her favorite place to nap.

Quiet by design

  • Calm music for study, play, unwind and sleep.
  • Works offline. Everything is saved on your device, with no account.
  • No ads, and nothing to buy with real money. You start with 600 coins and earn more by focusing.
