mumeao — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Who we are: mumeao is made by Theerat Burusphat ("we"). Contact: theerat.burusphat@gmail.com.

The short version

mumeao keeps your cat, her room and your coins on your device. The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app does not collect, sell or share personal information about you. We have no copy of anything the app saves.

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply. We keep support emails for 12 months, then delete them.

What the app keeps on your device

The app saves its own state in your device's app storage so your cat is still there tomorrow:

  • your cat: her needs, temperament, trust, what she remembers, how old she is, and the daily routine she has learned (for example, when you usually feed her);
  • when you last had the app open, so she can react to how long you were away;
  • your Focus sessions: how many you have finished, how many you stopped today, and the gifts they brought;
  • your coins, what you have bought, and where you placed it;
  • your room: wall color, wallpaper, floor, time of day, windows and curtains, and whether the box and decorations are out;
  • your music and cat-sound settings;
  • whether you have started a Focus session before, so the app stops showing a first-time hint;
  • on Android, a Focus-end notice waiting to be shown, until it fires or is canceled;
  • a backup copy of your cat and of your room, and any save the app could not read, set aside so nothing is lost;
  • whether the app's error screen has already appeared since you opened it, forgotten when the app closes.

On iPhone and Android the app keeps these in its own storage on the phone (its preferences: UserDefaults on iPhone, SharedPreferences on Android), which the phone does not clear on its own.

None of this is sent to us, and we cannot see it. The app's own backup copies stay on the device; the app does not send them anywhere. Your device's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google's backup on Android) may include it and restore it when you reinstall; Apple or Google handle those backups under their terms, and you control them in your device settings.

What the app asks your phone for, and why

  • Notifications: to tell you a Focus session has finished if you left the app just before it ended. You can refuse, and the app still works.
  • Motion: so toys roll when you tilt the phone, and so the app knows when the phone is face down during Focus. Motion is read on the device, never stored or sent. The app reads it without showing a permission prompt, on both iPhone and Android.
  • Vibration: for the purr you feel while your cat naps, and for light taps.
  • Keep the screen awake: only while a Focus session is running, so she can keep napping.
  • Internet (Android): the app declares it, but this version does not use it. Everything the app needs, including its music and sounds, is inside the app.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or any advertising identifier.

Children

mumeao is rated for a general audience (4+). It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, because nothing leaves the device. If you believe a child has sent us personal information some other way (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Everything the app saved is on your device. To delete it, delete the app (on Android you can also clear the app's storage in Settings). If your device's own backup holds a copy, delete that backup in your device settings too. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.

Security

Your cat's data stays on your device, protected by your device's own security.

Do Not Track

The app does not track you across apps or websites, so it has nothing to change when a Do Not Track signal or Apple's App Tracking Transparency setting is on.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with data, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change reaches you, and where the law requires, we will ask for your consent first.

Technical appendix (for reviewers)

Storage keys the app writes on the device:

KeyWhat it holds
loaf.saveThe cat: needs, traits, trust, memory tags, personality, treat count, age and learned routine; when the app was last open; the box and decorations; Focus counts, today's stopped sessions and past gifts. A backup copy is kept as loaf.save.backup. A damaged save is set aside on the device as loaf.save.bad (older ones as .bad.1 and .bad.2), and a too-new one as loaf.save.too_new
loaf.furnishings.v1Coins, what was bought, and where it is placed. A backup copy is kept as loaf.furnishings.v1.backup, and a damaged one is set aside as loaf.furnishings.v1.bad (older ones as .bad.1 and .bad.2)
loaf.toy-arrivals.v1For a new player's room: which of its toys have arrived so far, and when the last session began
loaf.room-style.v1Wall color, wallpaper and floor choices
loaf.room-time.v1The room's time-of-day setting
loaf.windows.v1Which windows and curtains are open
loaf.music.v1Music choice and volume
loaf.cat-voice.v1Cat sound on/off and volume
loaf.focus-triedWhether a Focus session was ever started
loaf.native-storage.v1Whether earlier saves have been moved into the phone's own storage
loaf.fatal-shownSession storage only: whether the error screen appeared since the app was opened; forgotten when it closes
loaf.deviceIdReserved for a feature this version does not use; never created by this version

The Android notifications plugin also keeps a scheduled Focus-end notice in the app's own SharedPreferences until it fires.

Android permissions (the app's, Capacitor's and its plugins'):

  • android.permission.INTERNET: declared by the app; unused in this version.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: the Focus-end notice.
  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: declared by the notifications plugin; the app schedules only inexact notices.
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: keeps a scheduled notice after a restart.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: the notifications plugin.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: the purr and taps.

iOS usage descriptions:

  • NSMotionUsageDescription: tilt and face-down detection (the app's shell grants the web view's motion request itself, so no prompt appears).
  • NSUserNotificationsUsageDescription: present in Info.plist for the Focus-end notice; iOS shows its own notification prompt instead.

Other facts:

  • Network: the app loads only files bundled in it and makes no network requests.
  • Third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or attribution: none.
  • Sounds and music: all bundled in the app.
