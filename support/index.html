Common questions

Do I need an account?

Everything is saved on your device, with no account.

How does Focus work?

Start a Focus session and she walks over, curls up on your phone and falls asleep. Keep the app open, put the phone face down and let her be.

Why did she wake up?

Leave the app or stop early and she wakes up and gives you a look.

Can I buy anything?

No ads, and nothing to buy with real money. You start with 600 coins and earn more by focusing.

How the app treats your data is in the privacy policy, and the rules for using it in the terms of use.
